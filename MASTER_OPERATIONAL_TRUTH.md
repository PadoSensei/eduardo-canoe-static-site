## Chapter 3: System Rules & Timeouts (Operational logic)

Technical logic translated into business rules for operational clarity.

### 3.1 The 15-Minute Rule

- **Logic:** Once a booking is initiated, the guest has exactly **15 minutes** to complete the Pix payment.
- **Enforcement:** The QR code expires after this window. If payment is not detected, the reserved spot is released back to the general inventory.

### 3.2 The 19:00 Lock (Iron Shield)

- **Logic:** At **19:00 (America/Fortaleza)**, bookings for the following day are automatically closed.
- **Enforcement:**
  - Tour status changes to "Closed" or "Manifest Finalized" on the UI.
  - The Daily Manifest Report is generated and emailed to the operational team.
  - No further online bookings are permitted for that date.

### 3.3 Support Information

- **Official Support Email:** `contato@pipacanoahavaiana.com.br`
- **Official Meeting Point:** Tibau do Sul, RN (Lagoa das Guaraíras).
